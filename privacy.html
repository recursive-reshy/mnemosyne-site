Mnemosyne privacy policy. Mnemosyne is a personal app used only by its owner, Naresh, and runs on his own computer. With his permission, it reads his Gmail with read-only access to collect selected newsletters and job-alert emails. Email content is stored only on his own computer, and parts of it are sent to Anthropic’s Claude API to produce summaries and extract job details. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account (Security → Third-party connections) or from Mnemosyne’s Settings. Contact: reshs92.09@gmail.com.
